# Phase 06 — Knowledge Domains

Status: **PLANNED**

## Objective
Use useful general knowledge as puzzle material while keeping cognition—not trivia—the primary game.

## Domains
### Mathematics
Age-appropriate counting, comparison, arithmetic, number patterns, estimation, ratios/percentages at older levels, and practical reasoning.

### Nature and General Science
Animals, plants, habitats, weather, seasons, ecosystems, simple human-body knowledge, basic physics, Earth and space.

### Problem Solving
Routes, ordering, limited moves, cause/effect, constraints, planning and deduction. This is a major domain, not an occasional category.

### Language and Literature
For younger players: vocabulary, picture/word matching, rhyme, sentence/story order.
For older players: original short reading passages, context meaning, analogy, narrative order, motives and interpretation.
Do not reproduce copyrighted literature. Use original text or suitable public-domain material.

### History
Prefer chronology, before/after, cause/effect, ancient/modern classification, historical objects, major civilizations and major world milestones over memorizing exact dates.

## Core rule
Do not make MIND VAULT a school quiz.

Bad: direct fact recall as the default.
Good: use knowledge inside matching, sequencing, classification, deduction, memory or problem-solving tasks.

## Content depth
Teach/expose useful concepts up to a sensible general level. Do not attempt deep curriculum completeness.

## Quality over quantity
Start with a small curated content set. Do not generate hundreds of low-quality questions simply to increase count.
